# BlueOak Software
## What we're building
A premium marketing site that helps startups and growing businesses understand BlueOak's software services and start a project.
## Who reads this
Founders and operators choosing a product development partner.
## Pages
- Home — services, work examples, process, proof, FAQ and project inquiry.
## Look & feel
Deep navy with the BlueOak royal/sky blues, precise technical grid and restrained oak-arc motifs; premium and calm.
## Login & data
No login; the contact form is a front-end inquiry mockup until a real destination is provided.
## Assumptions
- The supplied brand brief is the source of truth; no logo asset is present in the workspace.
- Case studies, testimonials, stats and client names are illustrative examples to replace before publication.
- Pricing will be proposed from researched U.S. software-agency benchmarks.
